1. Executive Summary & Architectural Alignment

01

Following the target architecture proposed in the client's presentation, this document outlines how the vision of "One Platform, Distinct Banking Environments" is fully realized in the live platform. Central administration maintains governance over shared products, licenses, and global templates, while granting each banking tenant dedicated administration over its employees, managers, customized policies, skill assessment exams, and private audit trails.

Core Architectural Principle: Logical Multi-Tenancy (Tier 1)
All bank tenants share a single high-performance codebase and database infrastructure while maintaining strict, server-enforced data boundaries. Every database query, user session, policy, quiz, and log entry is strictly scoped by business_id. Zero cross-tenant data leakage is permitted.

2B. Architecture & Infrastructure Tier Analysis

02B

In Slide 9 and Slide 10 of the client proposal, three potential infrastructure tiers were presented: Tier 1 (Logical Isolation), Tier 2 (Dedicated Database per bank), and Tier 3 (Dedicated deployment cluster per bank). This section explains the engineering rationale and why Tier 1 was implemented.

Architecture Decision: Why Tier 1 Logical Multi-Tenancy is Implemented

Tier 1 (Logical Isolation) is the industry-standard architecture adopted by leading enterprise platforms (e.g. Salesforce, Workday, Stripe). It achieves 100% data confidentiality while avoiding the massive operational overhead, reliability risks, and costs of running hundreds of isolated database instances.

Infrastructure Tier Comparison Matrix

Dimension Tier 1: Logical Isolation (Delivered) Tier 2: Dedicated Database (Roadmap) Tier 3: Dedicated Cluster (Enterprise)
Data Isolation Server-enforced via business_id across all tables and queries. Zero data leakage. Separate physical database per bank; separate connection pools. Completely isolated VPS/Kubernetes cluster per bank.
Hosting & Cost Optimal. Low footprint, efficient resource sharing, easily scalable. High. 300-400% cost increase due to multiple database engines. Extreme. 800%+ cost increase; requires dedicated DevOps team.
Database Migrations Instantaneous. Single atomic migration updates schema across all tenants. Complex. Must iterate through dozens of databases; failure risk during upgrades. Manual. Multi-cluster CI/CD pipeline required for every release.
Platform Maintenance Central updates, unified bug fixing, zero downtime deployments. Requires database sharding middleware and dynamic connection switching. Requires 24/7 infrastructure monitoring per cluster.
Suitability Immediate Production Ready (Recommended) Future tier for high-compliance enterprise banks requiring dedicated DB. Contractually mandated government / sovereign banking deployments.

Security Guarantees of Tier 1 Implementation

1. Server-Side Scoping, Never UI Filtering

Every Eloquent query in controllers and repositories automatically appends where('business_id', $businessId). Tampering with client parameters or URLs cannot access another bank's records.

2. Separate Middleware Authentication

The business portal is protected by business.auth, which uses a dedicated session guard isolated from standard web users and central administrators.

3. Dual Audit Logging

Platform events are recorded in AdminActivityLog. Bank events are stored in BusinessActivityLog with strict tenant scoping. Banks cannot inspect each other's audit trails.

4. Future Migration Readiness

Because database interactions are decoupled through Repository classes, routing a specific enterprise client to a separate physical database in future phases requires zero frontend code rewriting.

6. Dual-Audit System & Bank Contact Us Routing

06

Financial compliance mandates an immutable, timestamped record of administrative and user actions. The platform maintains two segregated audit logging systems: one for central platform governance and one confidential log per bank tenant.

Super Admin Master Activity Logs & Audit Trail /activity-logs
Operator Instructions: Audit Trail Architecture & Dual Segregation Model
  1. Central Platform Audit (AdminActivityLog): Records platform-level events performed by Super Admins: tenant creation, subscription adjustments, status changes (Active/Suspended), and master template updates.
  2. Bank Tenant Audit (BusinessActivityLog): Dedicated to the authenticated bank tenant (/business/activity-logs). Captures staff creation, user registration approvals, policy revisions, and candidate exam evaluations. Banks cannot inspect each other's audit trails.
  3. Audit Details & Immutability: Every log entry permanently stores the exact timestamp, operator name/email, target module, action description, and source IP address.

6B. Support & Inquiries Management: Bank vs. Central Routing

06B

The platform implements intelligent support inquiry routing, ensuring bank employee queries reach their internal support team while general user inquiries route to central support.

Inquiry Management & Support Inbox /contacts/index (Central) & /business/contacts (Tenant)
Operator Instructions: Support Routing Mechanics & Resolution
  1. Tenant-Specific Support Routing: When a bank specifies an official contact_email in its profile settings, support tickets submitted by its employees route directly to the bank's administrative portal inbox (/business/contacts).
  2. Central Support Fallback: General platform inquiries or messages from non-affiliated users route to the Super Admin support console (/contacts/index) for resolution.
  3. Email Reply & Status Tracking: Support personnel can inspect the inquiry message, sender contact details, and trigger an email response directly from the platform interface.
